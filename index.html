Cut-out method

To replace the environment the person has to be cut out. That works offline when the person stands in front of a plain wall or cloth (a green or blue cloth is best for lively poses). In an ordinary room use the AI cut-out (needs internet once).

Cut-out fine-tuning

If an outline looks too thick raise Edge trim; if parts of the person (hands, shoes) are cut away lower Sensitivity.
